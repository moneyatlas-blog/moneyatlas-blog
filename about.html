About MoneyAtlas

MoneyAtlas is where money meets geography.

We publish long-form explainers on the money stories hiding inside world maps: which countries are rich, why they got rich, how tax havens work, and where the world's wealth really lives. Every article is built on public data — primarily the World Bank's GDP figures — and written for curious readers, not economists.

MoneyAtlas is the companion site to the MoneyMaps and WealthAtlas video channels, which tell the same stories in short cinematic form.

What we cover

  • - Rich countries, explained — Switzerland, Singapore, Norway, Qatar, Luxembourg and more: how they actually built their wealth.
  • - The offshore world — tax havens, shell companies, and the trillions hiding in plain sight.
  • - Money and power — sovereign wealth funds, oil fortunes, financial disasters, and the people behind them.

Our approach

  • - Real figures from public sources, rounded for readability.
  • - No invented statistics — when a number is an estimate, we say so.
  • - Written in plain English for a general audience.
